## Exploratory Data Analysis
[Education & Big Five scores plots](EducationtoBFI)

When comparing Big Five personality scores across education levels, little variation was observed for any trait (Extraversion, Agreeableness, Conscientiousness, Neuroticism, and Openness). Average Neuroticism scores were lower at higher education levels, but by about a third of a point or less on the 6-point scale, which is small. Average Openness scores were higher at higher education levels, but again by about a third of a point or less on the 6-point scale. Overall, the group averages look fairly similar across education levels, suggesting that education level was only weakly related to Big Five scores.
